    Why Tone Matters in Clinical Idoma

    Idoma uses relative pitch levels (High, Mid, Low) to distinguish words that share identical letters. Misplacing tone can alter meaning completely. Always pair written prompts with approved audio recordings, clear gestures, and respectful greetings.

    Ebi — Blood vs. Feces vs. To be dark (pitch-dependent)
    Ipu — Abdomen / Stomach vs. Inside / Within
    Akpà — Skin vs. Bag / Pocket
