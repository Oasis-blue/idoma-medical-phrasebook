    Why Tone & Dialects Matter in Clinical Idoma

    Idoma uses relative pitch levels (High, Mid, Low) to distinguish words that share identical letters, and spans regional dialects including Otukpo (Central), Alala (Orokam), Agatu, Otukpa, and Apa. Always pair written prompts with approved audio recordings, clear gestures, and respectful greetings.
